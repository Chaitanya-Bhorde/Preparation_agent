# Placement-Focused Features Implementation Plan

## High-Impact Features for Campus Placements

### 1. Mock Interview Simulator (AI-Powered)
- Technical + HR question bank
- Timed sessions with countdown timer
- AI-generated feedback and scoring
- Session history and progress tracking
- Company-specific interview patterns

### 2. Aptitude Test Module
- Quantitative, Logical, Verbal sections
- Timed tests with auto-submit
- Detailed performance analytics
- Topic-wise weakness identification
- Previous test history

### 3. Company Preparation Roadmap
- Step-by-step guided preparation for each company
- Topic prioritization based on company patterns
- Progress tracking with milestones
- Estimated preparation time

### 4. Daily Challenge System
- New problem every day at midnight
- Streak tracking with rewards
- Special badges (7-day, 30-day, 100-day)
- Daily leaderboard

### 5. Leaderboard & Peer Comparison
- Overall ranking by total solved
- Topic-wise ranking
- Company-wise ranking
- Weekly/monthly leaderboards

### 6. Interview Experience Sharing
- Share interview experiences
- Browse by company
- Upvote/downvote helpful posts
- Tag by role and year

### 7. PWA Support
- Installable on mobile/desktop
- Offline access to saved content
- Push notifications for daily challenges

### 8. Progress Export (PDF)
- Export preparation progress as PDF
- Include stats, solved problems, ATS score
- Ready to attach to resume/portfolio
